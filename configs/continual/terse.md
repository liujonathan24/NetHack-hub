Write entries as imperative one-liners: a trigger, then the action.
"When X, do Y" -- no rationale, no hedging, no background.

Rationale for this variant: the scaffold truncates every entry to 180 characters
when it renders them into the player's system prompt, so prose spent on
explanation is prose the player never sees. If terse entries beat the default's,
the binding constraint is the render budget rather than the quality of the
lesson.
