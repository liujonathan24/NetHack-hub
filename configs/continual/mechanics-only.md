Only persist entries about GAME MECHANICS the player got wrong -- what a tool
does, what a prompt expects, which action is legal when. Do not persist strategy
("descend faster", "fight less"), pacing advice, or anything that is a judgement
call rather than a fact about the game.

Rationale for this variant: E8 found the model's reasoning accurate but inert --
it narrates well and then does not change what it executes. If mechanical facts
help and strategic advice does not, the deficit is knowledge; if neither helps,
it is not a knowledge problem at all.
